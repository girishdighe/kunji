Corner readout — eight studies

Each is self-contained: one <canvas>, a few dozen lines of JS, no network, no assets — so any of them drops straight into the single-file build. Hit Use to load one into the mock above.

Wiring the chosen one into dist/kunji.html: it’s an inline <canvas> plus a requestAnimationFrame loop — no fetch, no external font, no storage — so it clears the CSP and the no-network invariant scan untouched. The 7-segment TOTP countdown reads real wall-clock time and lines up with Kunji’s live TOTP tab; the rest are decorative but honest about what they depict.
